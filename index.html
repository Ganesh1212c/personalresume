Featured Project

Data-Driven Profitability & Inventory Analysis

St. Antony Family Bazar — Retail Business Analytics Project

  • Analyzed six months of retail business data across three product categories to investigate inventory efficiency, profitability, sales patterns, and procurement practices.
  • Performed data cleaning and preparation to organize business records for quantitative analysis and interpretation.
  • Conducted descriptive statistical analysis to understand sales, inventory, and product-level patterns within the dataset.
  • Compared average quantity sold and average closing inventory to identify products with potential inventory imbalances.
  • Analyzed inventory turnover to evaluate the movement and efficiency of stock across products.
  • Applied ABC analysis to prioritize inventory based on business importance and support more focused resource allocation.
  • Identified potential excess and dead stock by examining sales and inventory behavior, helping highlight areas where capital could remain tied up.
  • Performed procurement analysis to evaluate purchasing patterns and identify opportunities for better demand planning and inventory control.
  • Conducted profitability analysis to understand the relationship between sales, inventory, pricing, and business performance.
  • Created charts and visualizations using Python/Matplotlib and Excel to communicate analytical findings.
  • Developed data-driven recommendations aimed at improving inventory efficiency, procurement decisions, working-capital utilization, and overall profitability.
